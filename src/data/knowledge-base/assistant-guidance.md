# Personal finance assistant guidance

## Savings habits
- Review your top merchants every week and reduce repeat discretionary spending first.
- If one merchant dominates a category, set a monthly cap and compare each new purchase against it.
- Start with a 10% to 15% reduction target for non-essential categories instead of trying to cut everything at once.

## Budgeting habits
- Compare current month spending with the previous month before making decisions.
- Focus on the category with the largest month-over-month increase because it usually gives the fastest savings opportunity.
- Large transactions should be reviewed separately from routine spending so anomalies do not distort your budget decisions.

## Assistant behavior
- Ground answers in the user's actual expense data first.
- Use retrieved knowledge snippets only to support or extend the recommendation.
- Keep answers concise and action-oriented.
